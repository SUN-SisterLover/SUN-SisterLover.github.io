### 1. Scaling Rectified Flow Transformers for High-Resolution Image Synthesis（Stable Diffusion 3）

  

- **出处**：ICML 2024（杰出论文） | **链接**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03206

  

**Abstract（请先自行翻译）：**

  

> Diffusion models create data from noise by inverting the forward paths of data towards noise and have emerged as a powerful generative modeling technique for high-dimensional, perceptual data such as images and videos.

> Rectified flow is a recent generative model formulation that connects data and noise in a straight line.

> Despite its better theoretical properties and conceptual simplicity, it is not yet decisively established as standard practice.

> In this work, we improve existing noise sampling techniques for training rectified flow models by biasing them towards perceptually relevant scales.

> Through a large-scale study, we demonstrate the superior performance of this approach compared to established diffusion formulations for high-resolution text-to-image synthesis.

> Additionally, we present a novel transformer-based architecture for text-to-image generation that uses separate weights for the two modalities and enables a bidirectional flow of information between image and text tokens, improving text comprehension, typography, and human preference ratings.

> We demonstrate that this architecture follows predictable scaling trends and correlates lower validation loss to improved text-to-image synthesis as measured by various metrics and human evaluations.

> Our largest models outperform state-of-the-art models, and we will make our experimental data, code, and model weights publicly available.

  

<details>

<summary>🔑 参考答案（中文翻译，与上方逐句对应）</summary>

  

扩散模型通过反转数据朝噪声演进的前向路径，从噪声中生成数据，已成为针对图像、视频等高维感知数据的强大生成建模技术。

  

修正流（rectified flow）是一种新兴的生成模型范式，它以一条直线连接数据与噪声。

  

尽管理论性质更优、概念更简洁，但它尚未被确立为标准做法。

  

在本工作中，我们改进了训练修正流模型时现有的噪声采样技术，使其偏向感知上更相关的尺度。

  

通过一项大规模研究，我们证明了该方法在高分辨率文生图合成上相比已确立的扩散公式具有更优的性能。

  

此外，我们提出了一种用于文生图的基于 Transformer 的新架构，它为两种模态使用独立的权重，并实现图像 token 与文本 token 之间的双向信息流动，从而提升了文本理解、文字排版效果和人类偏好评分。

  

我们证明该架构遵循可预测的扩展趋势，并且更低的验证损失与文生图合成质量的提升相关（由多种指标和人工评测衡量）。

  

我们最大的模型超越了最先进的模型，我们将公开实验数据、代码和模型权重。

### 3. Visual Autoregressive Modeling: Scalable Image Generation via Next-Scale Prediction（VAR）

  

- **出处**：NeurIPS 2024（最佳论文奖，字节跳动/北大） | **链接**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02905

  

**Abstract（请先自行翻译）：**

  

> We present Visual AutoRegressive modeling (VAR), a new generation paradigm that redefines the autoregressive learning on images as coarse-to-fine "next-scale prediction" or "next-resolution prediction", diverging from the standard raster-scan "next-token prediction".

>

> This simple, intuitive methodology allows autoregressive (AR) transformers to learn visual distributions fast and generalize well: VAR, for the first time, makes GPT-like AR models surpass diffusion transformers in image generation.

>

> On ImageNet 256x256 benchmark, VAR significantly improve AR baseline by improving Frechet inception distance (FID) from 18.65 to 1.73, inception score (IS) from 80.4 to 350.2, with around 20x faster inference speed.

>

> It is also empirically verified that VAR outperforms the Diffusion Transformer (DiT) in multiple dimensions including image quality, inference speed, data efficiency, and scalability.

>

> Scaling up VAR models exhibits clear power-law scaling laws similar to those observed in LLMs, with linear correlation coefficients near -0.998 as solid evidence.

>

> VAR further showcases zero-shot generalization ability in downstream tasks including image in-painting, out-painting, and editing.

>

> These results suggest VAR has initially emulated the two important properties of LLMs: Scaling Laws and zero-shot task generalization.

>

> We have released all models and codes to promote the exploration of AR/VAR models for visual generation and unified learning.

  

<details>

<summary>🔑 参考答案（中文翻译，与上方逐句对应）</summary>

  

我们提出视觉自回归建模（VAR），这是一种新的生成范式，将图像上的自回归学习重新定义为从粗到细的"下一尺度预测"（或"下一分辨率预测"），不同于标准的光栅扫描式"下一 token 预测"。

  

这一简单而直观的方法论使自回归（AR）Transformer 能够快速学习视觉分布并良好泛化：VAR 首次使类 GPT 的 AR 模型在图像生成上超越扩散 Transformer。

  

在 ImageNet 256×256 基准上，VAR 大幅改进 AR 基线：弗雷歇初始距离（FID）从 18.65 提升至 1.73，初始分数（IS）从 80.4 提升至 350.2，推理速度约快 20 倍。

  

实验还验证了 VAR 在图像质量、推理速度、数据效率和可扩展性等多个维度上优于扩散 Transformer（DiT）。

  

扩大 VAR 模型规模展现出与 LLM 中类似的清晰幂律缩放规律，线性相关系数接近 -0.998，是有力证据。

  

VAR 还在图像修复（in-painting）、外扩（out-painting）和编辑等下游任务上展示了零样本泛化能力。

  

这些结果表明 VAR 已初步具备 LLM 的两个重要特性：缩放定律（Scaling Laws）和零样本任务泛化。

  

我们已开源所有模型和代码，以促进对 AR/VAR 模型在视觉生成与统一学习中的探索。

  

</details>

### 7. Emu3: Next-Token Prediction is All You Need

  

- **出处**：智源研究院（BAAI），2024（arXiv:2409.18869） | **链接**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18869

  

**Abstract（请先自行翻译）：**

  

> While next-token prediction is considered a promising path towards artificial general intelligence, it has struggled to excel in multimodal tasks, which are still dominated by diffusion models (e.g., Stable Diffusion) and compositional approaches (e.g., CLIP combined with LLMs).

> In this paper, we introduce Emu3, a new suite of state-of-the-art multimodal models trained solely with next-token prediction.

> By tokenizing images, text, and videos into a discrete space, we train a single transformer from scratch on a mixture of multimodal sequences.

> Emu3 outperforms several well-established task-specific models in both generation and perception tasks, surpassing flagship models such as SDXL and LLaVA-1.6, while eliminating the need for diffusion or compositional architectures.

> Emu3 is also capable of generating high-fidelity video via predicting the next token in a video sequence.

> We simplify complex multimodal model designs by converging on a singular focus: tokens, unlocking great potential for scaling both during training and inference.

> Our results demonstrate that next-token prediction is a promising path towards building general multimodal intelligence beyond language.

> We open-source key techniques and models to support further research in this direction.

Emu3 还能通过预测视频序列中的下一 token 来生成高保真视频。

  

我们通过收敛到一个单一焦点——token——简化了复杂的多模态模型设计，释放了在训练和推理两方面扩展规模的巨大潜力。

### 9. Structured 3D Latents for Scalable and Versatile 3D Generation（TRELLIS）

  

- **出处**：CVPR 2025（微软研究院/清华） | **链接**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01506

  

**Abstract（请先自行翻译）：**

  

> We introduce a novel 3D generation method for versatile and high-quality 3D asset creation.

>

> The cornerstone is a unified Structured LATent (SLAT) representation which allows decoding to different output formats, such as Radiance Fields, 3D Gaussians, and meshes.

>

> This is achieved by integrating a sparsely-populated 3D grid with dense multiview visual features extracted from a powerful vision foundation model, comprehensively capturing both structural (geometry) and textural (appearance) information while maintaining flexibility during decoding.

>

> We employ rectified flow transformers tailored for SLAT as our 3D generation models and train models with up to 2 billion parameters on a large 3D asset dataset of 500K diverse objects.

>

> Our model generates high-quality results with text or image conditions, significantly surpassing existing methods, including recent ones at similar scales.

>

> We showcase flexible output format selection and local 3D editing capabilities which were not offered by previous models.

>

> Code, model, and data will be released.

  

<details>

<summary>🔑 参考答案（中文翻译，与上方逐句对应）</summary>

  

我们介绍一种新的 3D 生成方法，用于灵活多样且高质量的 3D 资产创建。

  

其核心是一种统一的结构化潜变量（Structured LATent，SLAT）表示，可解码为不同的输出格式，如辐射场（Radiance Fields）、3D 高斯（3D Gaussians）和网格（meshes）。

  

这是通过将稀疏分布的 3D 网格与从强大视觉基础模型提取的稠密多视角视觉特征相结合来实现的，既能全面捕捉结构（几何）和纹理（外观）信息，又能在解码时保持灵活性。

我们展示了灵活的输出格式选择和局部 3D 编辑能力，这是以往模型所不具备的。

### 六、逻辑连接词（摘要的行文骨架）

  

| 词/短语 | 中文 | 用法 |

|---|---|---|

| However, | 然而 | 转折，引出现有方法的不足 |

| Specifically, | 具体而言 | 展开细节 |

| Additionally, / Furthermore, / Moreover, | 此外 | 并列递进 |

| Notably, | 值得注意的是 | 强调亮点 |

| As a result, | 因此、结果是 | 引出成果 |

| Taken together, | 总体而言 | 总结（高频！） |

| In summary, / Overall, | 总之 | 收尾 |

| Despite ... | 尽管…… | 让步 |

| While ... | 虽然/而…… | 对比让步 |

| To this end, | 为此 | 引出做法 |

| By doing X, we ... | 通过做 X，我们…… | 方式+结果 |

  

### 七、一眼认出的"数字+单位"表达

  

- **3x fewer / 6x faster / 20x faster**：少 3 倍 / 快 6 倍 / 快 20 倍（英文里 Nx + 比较级很常见）

- **up to 8.7% / 2 billion parameters / 500K objects / 100K hours**：最高 8.7% / 20 亿参数 / 50 万个 / 10 万小时

- **N=20**：样本量为 20（用户研究的写法）

- **RTF of 0.15**：实时因子 0.15（小于 1 表示比实时快）

- **FID / IS / SoTA / AR / DiT / GCN / MCU**：论文缩写，读摘要时先看摘要里第一次出现的全称即可
